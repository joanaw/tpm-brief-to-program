# AI-Powered Customer Intelligence Initiative
## Leadership Brief — Q2 2026

**From:** Chief Product Officer
**To:** Program Management
**Date:** March 17, 2026

---

We want to leverage AI to get smarter about our customers. Right now we're leaving a lot of signal on the table — support tickets, usage data, churn patterns, NPS responses — and we're not connecting the dots fast enough. Competitors are moving and we need to move too.

The vision is something like a "customer intelligence layer" that gives our product, sales, and success teams a unified view of what customers actually need, before they have to ask. Ideally this would be proactive — flagging at-risk accounts, surfacing expansion opportunities, recommending next best actions. Maybe there's a dashboard, maybe it's embedded in existing tools, we're not sure yet. We should probably use AI agents somewhere in here.

We'd like to see something in market by Q3. The data team has been working on some related stuff already so there may be synergies there. Legal flagged something about GDPR a few weeks ago but I think it was resolved. Engineering is heads-down on the v2.0 release until end of April but they'll be available after that.

Budget is TBD but this is a priority. Let's make it happen.

— CPO
